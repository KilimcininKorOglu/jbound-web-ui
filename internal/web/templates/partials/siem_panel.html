How this works

Every audit entry leaves the panel as a CEF line on facility {{.Settings.Facility}}, tagged unbound-dns-panel.

The dhost field carries the DNS server the action was aimed at, and dvchost carries this panel. A change across a group therefore arrives as one line per server.

A copy is written to {{.Settings.LogFile}}, which is what the viewer below reads.
